Block 1 of the detector: give every pixel a score that is high on yellow paint and zero everywhere else. Source: labs/lanedet/mask.py.

Yellow = red + green, no bluemask.py · yellowness()

Paint is about (255, 209, 0). Asphalt is (31, 31, 31). Grey surfaces have equal channels, and yellow has high red and green with no blue. One subtraction separates them: min(R, G) − B.

min means a pixel scores only if red and green are both high. Red curb (200, 30, 30): lab score 0, GPU score 0.67, so its GPU mask is 0.55, which reads as paint.

Which colours survive?both formulas over every R, G

Each map covers every red (→) and green (↑) value at a fixed blue. A colour that survives is drawn in its own colour with brightness = its mask value. A rejected colour is black.

Neither formula rejects the orange cone (C sits inside both regions). The mask is not the last defence. The centre block (page 4) rejects the cone because it is not the right distance from the other line.

From score to mask: a ramp, not a cliffmask.py · yellow_mask(low, high)

Below low the mask is 0 (noise floor). Above high it is 1. In between it rises linearly. Edge pixels, only partly covered by paint, land on the slope and count partly. That moves line centres by fractions of a pixel (page 2).

The ramp is the main tuning knob for lighting. Lower ramp = sees dim paint, but lets more noise through.
