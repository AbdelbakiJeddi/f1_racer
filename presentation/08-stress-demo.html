The detector under the six stress conditions from labs/08_tune.ipynb: what breaks, which block breaks, and which knob fixes it. Every metric is a full 120-frame lap against ground truth, computed live in this page.

Six conditions at a glancedefault vs tuned ramp

Each card shows the stressed frame, its mask, and the lap metrics for the default mask ramp (0.15 → 0.40) and the tuned one (0.05 → 0.25).

Heading = e_heading RMSE over the lap. Paired = share of frames with both bands paired. Orange = heading error over 1.5° or paired under 50 %.

Two real failures, both in the mask block: glare washes the paint's yellowness down to ~0.24, and shadow darkens it to ~0.12. Curb, cone and noise are already absorbed by the colour score, the width-based pairing and the mass threshold.

Diagnosis: the paint sinks below the rampnear + far profiles · frame 60

Look at the band profiles, the input of the scan block. Under glare the bumps collapse towards the level line. Lowering the ramp lifts them back.

Lowering the ramp has a price: specks saturate sooner. 0.05 → 0.25 is the setting that stays under 2.5° heading error in glare, shadow and noise σ = 30 together.
