4 · The PPO clip

Make good actions more likely, but never trust one batch too much.

Plain policy gradient

Raise the probability of actions with positive advantage, lower the others. Problem: one noisy batch can push the policy very far, and the next data then comes from a broken policy.

The core risk. In supervised learning a bad step is fixed by the next batch. In RL a bad step spoils the next batch.

The ratio

ratio = π_new(a|s) / π_old(a|s) 1.0 unchanged 1.2 now 20 % more likely 0.8 20 % less likely

The clip

PPO rewards the change only while the ratio stays within 1 ± ε. Past that, pushing further earns nothing.

objective = min( ratio·A , clip(ratio, 1−ε, 1+ε)·A )
Read the flat part. Flat = zero gradient = stop pushing. It is only flat on the side that would reward going further. If the policy moved the wrong way, the gradient stays and pulls it back.

The full loss in rsl_rl

loss = surrogate + value_loss_coef · value_loss − entropy_coef · entropy 1.0 0.008
