3 · Value and advantage

A reward of 0.13 is great on a hard bend and poor on a straight. PPO needs the comparison.

Value: what to expect from here

The critic learns V(s): the return expected from state s. It is trained to predict what really happened (Loss/value is its error).

Advantage = what happened − what was expected. Positive: do that more. Negative: do it less.

One-step surprise: δ

δₜ = rₜ + γ·V(next) − V(now)

δ is the surprise of a single step. It is fast but trusts the critic completely.

Play with GAE

Eight steps. The car drives well, then drifts toward the line. Move λ and choose the ending.

Watch the ending. Failure: nothing comes after, so the bad ending leaks backwards and every advantage turns negative. Lower λ to keep the blame near the end. Time-out: the code adds γ·V so a good car is not punished because the clock stopped.
