Return and γ

The return is the sum of future rewards, each one step further counting a bit less:

G = r₀ + γ·r₁ + γ²·r₂ + γ³·r₃ + …

Orange: how much each future step counts (γᵗ). The green curve is what the car still collects before falling. Reward per good step = 4 × dt ≈ 0.133.

γ is how far the car looks. Horizon ≈ 1/(1−γ) steps. γ = 0.99 → 100 steps → 3.3 s of driving.

Why falling hurts, with no penalty at all

Your task has no explicit crash penalty. Ending the episode simply cuts the future: all remaining reward is lost.

Remember. A good driver is worth ≈ 13.3. The boundary penalty is at most 0.00024 per step: about 55 000 times smaller. The wall teaches, the warning is silent.

When positive rewards go wrong

Mostly positive

Staying alive pays. The car avoids ending the episode. Risk: it may crawl or stall to farm reward.

Mostly negative

Every step costs. Ending early can be the best choice: the car learns to crash quickly.
