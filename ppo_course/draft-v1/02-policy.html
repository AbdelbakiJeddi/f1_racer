2 · The policy

The actor does not output an action. It outputs a probability of actions.

A bell curve per action

The network gives a mean μ for steer and throttle. A separate learned number gives the std σ. During training the action is drawn at random around μ. In play mode, μ is used directly.

σ is exploration. Large: tries many things, drives noisily. Small: repeats what it knows, learns nothing new.
