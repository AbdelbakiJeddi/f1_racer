8 · Reward design

A reward is a specification. The agent will do exactly what it says, not what you meant.

Three families

Task

What you want: centering, progress. Large.

Safety

What must not happen: boundary, termination. Must be felt before the failure.

Style

How it should look: smoothness, rate. Small, or the car prefers not to act.

Shape: the centering bell

The slope teaches, not the height. Where the curve is flat, moving changes nothing, so the car gets no signal.

Case study: your boundary penalty

wheel_extent = distance of the outermost wheel corner from the centre line. It is about 0.14 m when centred. The episode ends at 0.56 m.

Try it. Set margin 0.40, weight 20, squared: the warning becomes about 70× louder. Linear is louder still, from the first centimetre.

Your fresh run solved the task without this change, because the centering term already keeps the car far from the lines. Change it only if a harder track shows wheel touches.
