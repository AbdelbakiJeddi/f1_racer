0 · The loop

Reinforcement learning is one loop, repeated 30 times per second.

Four words

The agent (a neural network) reads an observation, picks an action. The environment (Isaac Sim) moves the car and returns a reward and the next observation.

Remember. Nobody tells the car how to steer. It only gets a score. All behaviour comes from that score.

Two networks, two views

The actor drives. The critic only judges, during training. Your critic gets the exact simulator state (8 numbers), the actor only what the camera pipeline gives.

Remember. Cheating is allowed for the critic, never for the actor.

Episode

An episode starts at reset and ends when the car fails (a wheel touches a line, or rollover) or time runs out (40 s = 1200 steps). These two endings are treated differently: page 3.
